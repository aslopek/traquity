Before you enable AI features

TraQuity can optionally run a local AI model on this computer to assist with parts of the app. Please read the following before confirming.

Third-party weights, not TraQuity's own

The models offered here are published by third parties. TraQuity does not train, audit or control them, and is not responsible for what they generate.

A sizeable download, once you choose to start it

Downloading a model fetches a sizeable amount of data from Hugging Face, depending on which of the offered sizes you choose. Hugging Face sees your IP address for that download, and for nothing else. Nothing downloads until you explicitly start it.

Answers are unreliable and always require your own judgement

A local model can be wrong, out of date or misleading, the same as any other AI system. Never treat its output as fact without checking it yourself, and always confirm anything it suggests before acting on it.

Not financial advice

Neither TraQuity nor any AI model it runs is capable of providing financial advice. Every AI feature in this app is purely informational. Decisions about your investments remain entirely your own responsibility.
